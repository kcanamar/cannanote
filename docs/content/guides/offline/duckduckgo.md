# DuckDuckGo Offline Guide

DuckDuckGo's browser is built for privacy. Its aggressive data clearing features need some attention to protect your CannaNote journal.

---

## The Fire Button Warning

DuckDuckGo has a **Fire Button** 🔥 that clears all browsing data with one tap. This includes your journal entries.

### Protect CannaNote (Fireproof the Site)

**On Mobile:**
1. Visit **cannanote.app**
2. Tap the **⋮** menu
3. Tap **Fireproof This Site**
4. CannaNote data now survives the Fire Button

**On Desktop:**
1. Visit **cannanote.app**
2. Click the **Fire Button** 🔥
3. Before confirming, look for **"Fireproof This Site"**
4. Or: Right-click the Fire Button → Manage Fireproof Sites

### What Fireproofing Does

- Your journal entries survive when you use the Fire Button
- Cached pages and login session are preserved
- You stay signed in
- Other sites still get cleared normally

---

## Installing CannaNote

### On Mobile (iOS / Android)

1. Visit **cannanote.app** in DuckDuckGo
2. Tap the **⋮** menu
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. CannaNote appears as an app icon

*Note: DuckDuckGo mobile uses the system's WebView (WebKit on iOS, varies on Android), so PWA support depends on your device.*

### On Desktop (Mac)

DuckDuckGo for Mac supports adding sites:

1. Visit **cannanote.app**
2. Look for install/add options in the menu
3. Or bookmark it for quick access

---

## Managing Cached Data

### The Safe Approach

With DuckDuckGo, the safest approach is:

1. **Fireproof CannaNote** (see above)
2. Use the **Fire Button** freely for other browsing
3. Your journal stays intact

### Manual Cache Clearing

If you need to clear just CannaNote's cache:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Look for **Site settings** or **Website data**
3. Find **cannanote.app**
4. Clear cache while keeping site data

*Note: Options vary between DuckDuckGo mobile and desktop versions.*

---

## DuckDuckGo-Specific Notes

### Why the Fire Button Matters

DuckDuckGo is designed to leave no trace. That's great for privacy, but it means your journal gets wiped if you forget to fireproof. We strongly recommend fireproofing CannaNote immediately after first use.

### Privacy Alignment

CannaNote's privacy-first design works perfectly with DuckDuckGo:

- No trackers to block
- No third-party cookies
- All data stays local
- DuckDuckGo's protections enhance your privacy

### Email Protection

If you signed up for CannaNote using a DuckDuckGo email alias (@duck.com), everything works normally. We only send emails you've explicitly requested.

## Important Reminder

**Fireproof CannaNote before using the Fire Button.**

If you've already used the Fire Button without fireproofing, your journal data may be gone. If you had cloud sync enabled, sign back in to restore. If not, we're sorry - the data is unrecoverable.

Going forward: Fireproof first, Fire freely.
